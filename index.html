Cardio heart rate

Zone 2 is a steady, conversational pace that builds heart health and burns fat. With your resting heart rate, the range uses your heart rate reserve (more personal); without it, 60–70% of your estimated max (208 − 0.7 × age). If you take medication that affects heart rate, check this range with your doctor. These numbers stay on this device.
